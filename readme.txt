does what it says on the tin

thought about making it also convert the other way too, but then got bored.

run 'extract_and_convert.py' and drop in your deltarune.sav, and it'll spit out a deltarune.sav-PC folder for your use.
tested and works with PS4 saves up to chapter 5, only tested with switch saves up to chapter 2 because I don't own the full game for switch.
idk it's compatibility with other platforms.
